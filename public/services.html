General Dentistry

Our general dentistry services form the foundation of a healthy smile. Regular dental check-ups help detect problems early, preventing costly and complex treatments later. We provide thorough examinations, professional cleanings (scaling and polishing), cavity detection, and personalised oral hygiene guidance. We also offer fluoride treatments and dental sealants for children to protect developing teeth from decay.

Prevention is always better than cure — a visit every 6 months can save you from significant dental problems.

Dental Check-up Scaling & Polishing Fluoride Treatment Cavity Detection Oral Hygiene Guidance X-rays

Dental Surgery

Our dental surgical services are performed with the highest standards of care and precision. Whether it is a simple tooth extraction or a more involved minor oral surgical procedure, Dr. Sujata V Gaikwad ensures your comfort and safety throughout the process. We use local anaesthesia to make the experience as painless as possible.

Post-operative care instructions are provided to ensure a swift and smooth recovery. We are also available for follow-up consultations after surgery.

Tooth Extraction Wisdom Tooth Removal Minor Oral Surgery Local Anaesthesia Post-op Care

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Root Canal Treatment, often feared, is actually a comfortable and tooth-saving procedure when performed by an experienced dentist. At Jivak Dental Clinic, we use modern endodontic techniques to remove infected pulp tissue, clean and shape the root canals, and seal them to prevent reinfection.

The result? Your natural tooth is preserved, pain is eliminated, and you can eat and smile normally again. The procedure is typically completed in 1–3 visits depending on the severity of infection.

Pulp Removal Canal Cleaning Canal Shaping Bio-compatible Sealing Post-RCT Crown Infection Control
🪥

Tooth Restoration

Restore the function, aesthetics, and confidence of your smile with our tooth restoration services. Whether you have a cracked, broken, severely decayed, or missing tooth, we offer a range of restorative options including dental crowns, tooth-coloured fillings, bridges, and dentures.

We use high-quality materials that match the natural colour and texture of your teeth, ensuring restorations that look and feel completely natural. Our goal is to restore your smile while maintaining optimal bite function and oral health.

Dental Crowns Dental Bridges Inlays & Onlays Dentures Broken Tooth Repair Natural-Look Materials
✨

Cosmetic Filling (Composite Filling)

Say goodbye to dark, unsightly amalgam fillings! Our composite (tooth-coloured) fillings are made from a resin material that is custom-shaded to match your natural tooth colour exactly. They bond directly to your tooth, requiring minimal removal of tooth structure, and are virtually invisible once placed.

Cosmetic fillings are durable, safe, and mercury-free. They can be used to repair cavities, fix chipped or cracked teeth, and even replace old metal fillings for a more aesthetic appearance. The procedure is quick, usually completed in a single visit.

Tooth-coloured Composite Mercury-free Cavity Repair Chip Repair Replace Metal Fillings Single Visit
